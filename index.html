Nutritional Benchmarks

Dietary Reference Intakes (DRI): Protein, Vitamins & Minerals

Why Micronutrients Matter:

While protein builds muscle and tissue, Iron delivers oxygen to brain & cells, Calcium & Vit D build strong bones, Zinc & Vit C power immune defense, and Vitamin A supports eyesight and cellular growth.

Infant (6–12 months) ~11g Protein

~1.2g/kg protein. Crucial transition window where infant iron stores deplete and require complementary dietary iron.

🩸 Iron: 11 mg/day (Crucial!)
🦴 Calcium: 260 mg/day
🛡️ Zinc: 3 mg/day
🍊 Vitamin C: 50 mg/day
Baby Safety: Zero added salt or sugar. NO honey under 12 months (botulism risk). Safe textures: smooth purees or soft mashes.
Toddler (1–4 years) ~13–15g Protein

High energy expenditure per kg. Supports bone elongation, teeth formation, and motor development.

🩸 Iron: 7 mg/day
🦴 Calcium: 700 mg/day
🛡️ Zinc: 3 mg/day
🍊 Vitamin C: 15 mg/day
Textures: Graspable soft finger foods. Quarter whole grapes/cherry tomatoes to avoid choking hazards.
Child (4–12 years) ~19–34g Protein

Peak childhood growth, cognitive stamina for school, and skeletal bone mineral accrual.

🩸 Iron: 10 mg/day
🦴 Calcium: 1,000 mg/day
🛡️ Zinc: 5–8 mg/day
🍊 Vitamin C: 25–45 mg/day
Adult Women ~46–60g Protein

High iron requirement to offset monthly menstrual losses, plus bone calcium preservation.

🩸 Iron: 18 mg/day (Higher)
🦴 Calcium: 1,000 mg/day
🛡️ Zinc: 8 mg/day
🍊 Vitamin C: 75 mg/day
Adult Men ~56–80g Protein

Higher muscle mass maintenance, testosterone synthesis (zinc/magnesium), and cardiovascular support.

🩸 Iron: 8 mg/day
🦴 Calcium: 1,000 mg/day
🛡️ Zinc: 11 mg/day
🍊 Vitamin C: 90 mg/day
