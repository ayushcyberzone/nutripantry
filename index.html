Nutritional Benchmarks

Dietary Reference Intakes (DRI) by age & gender

Infant (6–12 months) ~11g / day

~1.2g per kg body weight. Primarily from breastmilk or formula, with soft complementary solids introduced.

Crucial Safety: Zero added salt/sugar. NO honey under 12 months (infant botulism risk). Safe textures: smooth purees or soft mashes.

Toddler (1–4 years) ~13–15g / day

~1.05g per kg body weight. Essential for rapid physical growth and neurological development.

Textures: Graspable soft finger foods. Quarter whole grapes/cherry tomatoes to avoid choking hazards.

Child (4–12 years) ~19–34g / day

~0.95g per kg body weight. Support sustained activity, cognitive function, and bone density.

Adult Women ~46–60g / day

Baseline 0.8g/kg up to 1.2–1.4g/kg for active lifestyle (+25g during pregnancy/lactation).

Adult Men ~56–80g / day

Baseline 0.8g/kg up to 1.4–1.7g/kg for resistance training and muscle preservation.
